STYLE (keep identical across all images):
Game art for "Void Blaster", a top-down mobile space shooter (survivors-like).
Camera: top-down orthographic, looking straight down, no perspective.
Look: stylized sci-fi with a neon accent. Dark, rich materials (gunmetal, carbon,
matte ceramic) lit from the top-left, glowing edges and emissive lights only where
noted. Detail level: high but clean - panel lines, vents, rivets; no grunge overload.
The background must stay dark and calm so bright game objects read on top of it:
no bright cyan anywhere (cyan belongs to the player), magenta only as thin traces.
Rendering: crisp edges, soft bloom on glowing parts only, no motion blur, no depth of
field, not photorealistic. No text, no logo, no watermark.

#0d0a12 with facets #3b2a4a and an ember glow #ff5a2e inside, magenta #ff2d95 veins in

dust. Mood: unease, a planet being converted. 80% machine, crystal growths.
Mars is dusty and matte, never bright orange.
