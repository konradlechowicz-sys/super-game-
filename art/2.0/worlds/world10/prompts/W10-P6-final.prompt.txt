STYLE (keep identical across all images):
Game art for "Void Blaster", a top-down mobile space shooter (survivors-like).
Camera: top-down orthographic, looking straight down, no perspective.
Look: stylized sci-fi with a neon accent. Dark, rich materials (gunmetal, carbon,
matte ceramic) lit from the top-left, glowing edges and emissive lights only where
noted. Detail level: high but clean - panel lines, vents, rivets; no grunge overload.
The background must stay dark and calm so bright game objects read on top of it:
no bright cyan anywhere (cyan belongs to the player), magenta only as thin traces.
Rendering: crisp edges, soft bloom on glowing parts only, no motion blur, no depth of
field, not photorealistic. No text, no logo, no watermark.

WORLD 10 "THE NEXUS": the heart of the Void Collective (an alien machine intelligence):
a living mind of circuits and neurons where geometry stops making sense. Circuit traces
branch like dendrites, neuron nodes, floating impossible shapes (frames and stairs that
lead nowhere), broken rings orbiting. Darkness #000000/#0a0414, dominant violet #b04dff
(dark #3a1060), magenta #ff2d95 everywhere a little, tiny white sparks. Everything
glows from inside in pulses, like a heartbeat; between pulses the world is dark.
Mood: the finale, awe and dread. Full fusion of machine and organism.

TASK: Arena border pieces seen from directly above on a plain flat dark background
(#05010f), well apart: 4 straight strips (top, bottom, left, right), each 1024 px long
and about 160 px thick, tileable along their length: a dense tangle of neurons, circuit
cables and broken ring segments, faint violet glow, clean readable inner edge. Plus 4
matching corners 256x256.
