VOID BLASTER

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

This policy covers the game Void Blaster: Neon Survivor for iPhone (App Store) and Android (Google Play), made by Konrad Lechowicz, an independent developer (“we”).

Short version: Void Blaster does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your game progress stays on your device.

Data we collect

None. The game does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it does not send any information about you or your device to us or to third parties.

Data stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves the following on your device only:

  • cleared sectors and earned stars,
  • coins, purchased upgrades and unlocked ships,
  • the daily reward streak,
  • settings such as sound, music, vibration and controls.

This data never leaves your device. Deleting the app removes it. You can also erase it at any time in Settings → Reset progress inside the game.

Third-party services

Apart from the store services below (purchases and leaderboards), the game contains no third-party advertising, analytics, crash reporting or social SDKs, and it does not need an internet connection to play. All fonts, graphics and sounds are included in the app.

Purchases

The game offers one optional in-app purchase, the full game. Payment is handled entirely by the store you bought the game from: Apple through the App Store on iPhone, Google through Google Play Billing on Android. We never receive your name, Apple ID, Google account, card or payment details. The game only asks the store whether the purchase is owned on your account so it can unlock the content. In-game coins are earned only by playing and cannot be bought. Apple's and Google's handling of payment data is described in their own privacy policies (apple.com/legal/privacy, policies.google.com/privacy).

Leaderboards

The optional Endless leaderboards use Apple Game Center on iPhone and Google Play Games on Android. If you are signed in to that service, your score (the number of kills) and your player name there are sent to Apple or Google to show the leaderboard; we do not receive them. You can play without signing in.

Children

Because the game collects no personal data, it does not knowingly collect information from children of any age.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever starts handling personal data, this page will be updated before that change ships, and the effective date above will change.

Contact

Developer: Konrad Lechowicz, Poland. Questions about this policy? Write to the contact email shown on the game's App Store or Google Play page, or open an issue at github.com/konradlechowicz-sys/super-game-/issues.
