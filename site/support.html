VOID BLASTER

Support

Neon space survivor for iPhone. Steer with the joystick, your weapons fire on their own.

Need help or found a bug? Open an issue at github.com/konradlechowicz-sys/super-game-/issues and describe what happened, which sector you were in and your iPhone model.

How do I play?

Hold the joystick at the bottom of the screen to fly. Weapons fire automatically at the nearest enemy. Collect the crystals enemies drop to level up, then pick one of three upgrades. Survive until the timer runs out, or defeat the boss in the last sector of each world.

Can I change the controls?

Yes. Open Settings → Controls to switch between the fixed joystick, a floating joystick that appears under your thumb, and dragging anywhere on the screen.

A sector is too hard

Spend coins in Upgrades. Weapon Core and Reinforced Hull help the most, and Phoenix Protocol gives you one revive per run. Replaying earlier sectors earns more coins.

How are stars earned?

Each sector has three stars: clear the sector, reach the kill target, and finish with at least half of your hull.

How do I unlock the next world?

Defeat the boss in the sixth sector of the current world. Worlds 2–4 are part of the full game.

What is free, and what does the full game add?

The whole first world, including its boss, is free. A single purchase unlocks three more worlds with 18 sectors and three bosses, the Phantom and Titan ships, every upgrade level, 1,000 coins and the Gold Striker skin. There are no ads and no subscriptions.

I bought the full game but it is locked on my new iPhone

Open Settings → Restore purchases in the game while signed in with the same Apple ID. Family Sharing is supported.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is saved on your iPhone automatically. It is removed only if you delete the app or choose Settings → Reset progress.
